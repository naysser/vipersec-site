About

Built on real-world results

Vipersec was founded in 2025 by security researchers with hands-on experience discovering and responsibly disclosing critical vulnerabilities in enterprise platforms.

We focus on high-impact findings — SQL injection, server-side request forgery, broken access control, and authentication bypass — across Fortune 500 companies, financial institutions, and technology platforms.

Every vulnerability we report includes a fully reproducible proof of concept, raw HTTP evidence, and clear remediation steps. We don't report theoretical risks.
